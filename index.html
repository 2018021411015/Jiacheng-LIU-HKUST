I’m a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. In the Spintronic Quantum Material Laboratory (SQML), under the mentorship of Prof. Qiming Shao, I focus on hybrid systems based on magnon-spintronics (magnonics).From November 2023 to June 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Quantum Nano-Scale Magnetism Research Team at RIKEN, advised by Prof. Yoshichika Otani. My research combines device physics, micromagnetic and numerical modelling, microwave measurements, and nanofabrication to explore the dynamics of hybrid magnonic systems coupling magnons with photons, phonons, and superconducting qubits. By understanding and controlling these coherent interactions, I aim to harness magnetic materials as low-loss intermediaries for on-chip information transduction across different physical platforms. I am also interested in exploiting nonlinear spin-wave dynamics for energy-efficient and adaptive computing.

Current research interests

  • Spin Waves as Coherent Sources for Qubit ControlHarnessing the nonlinear response of magnetic materials to generate coherent spin-wave signals for the control of quantum bits.
  • Strong Coupling in Magnonic Hybrid SystemsStrong coupling in magnonic hybrid systems provides a tunable, low-power, and highly reconfigurable platform for information exchange, with potential applications ranging from microwave signal processing for 5G communications to cryogenic control of quantum bits. My research explores both dipolar-interaction-mediated magnon–photon coupling and interfacial-exchange-driven magnon–magnon coupling.
  • Magnetoelastic Artificial SuperlatticesBand engineering in artificial lattices enables tailored magnon–phonon coupling, providing a versatile platform for exploring emergent physical phenomena and realizing multifunctional control of surface acoustic waves.
