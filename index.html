Ph.D. Researcher · HKUST

Jiacheng Liu

Electronic and Computer Engineering · Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

I am a Ph.D. student advised by Prof. Qiming Shao. My research focuses on hybrid quantum systems involving magnons, photons, phonons, and superconducting qubits, with an emphasis on coherent coupling, low-dissipation devices, and on-chip quantum information processing.

From November 2023 to June 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Quantum Nano-Scale Magnetism Research Team at RIKEN, Japan, advised by Prof. Yoshichika Otani and Prof. Qiming Shao.

Seeking postdoctoral opportunities starting in 2027
Research

Current research interests

  • Hybrid magnon–photon systems
    Coherent microwave conversion and nonlinear magnonics.
  • Magnon–magnon coupling
    Strong coupling in low-dissipation magnetic heterostructures.
  • Magnon–phonon crystals
    Periodic structures for engineering hybrid excitations.
  • Quantum-device interfaces
    Magnon coupling with superconducting qubits and acoustic devices.
