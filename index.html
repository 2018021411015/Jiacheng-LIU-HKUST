I’m a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. In the Spintronic Quantum Material Laboratory (SQML), under the mentorship of Prof. Qiming Shao, I focus on hybrid quantum systems involving magnons, photons, phonons, and superconducting qubits.

From November 2023 to June 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Quantum Nano-Scale Magnetism Research Team at RIKEN, advised by Prof. Yoshichika Otani and Prof. Qiming Shao. My research combines device physics, micromagnetic and numerical modelling, microwave measurements, and nanofabrication to develop coherent, low-dissipation, on-chip quantum technologies.

Current research interests

  • Hybrid magnon–photon quantum systemsCoherent microwave conversion, nonlinear magnonic processes, and photon-mediated quantum interfaces.
  • Strong magnon–magnon couplingEngineering low-dissipation coupling in all-magnetic-insulator heterostructures through modelling, simulation, and experiment.
  • Hybrid magnon–phonon crystalsTailoring coupled excitations and band structures using periodic and moiré-like magnetic-acoustic systems.
  • Magnon–superconducting-qubit interfacesExploring on-chip hybrid platforms for coherent quantum control and information processing.

For a deeper insight into my research, please refer to my publications.

If you wish to discuss with me, feel free to contact me via email.
