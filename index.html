About me

Hybrid Quantum Systems, Magnonics & Spintronics

⌖ Hong Kong◷ Updated August 2026

I am a Ph.D. student in Electronic and Computer Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Qiming Shao.

My research focuses on hybrid quantum systems involving magnons, photons, phonons, and superconducting qubits. I am particularly interested in coherent coupling, low-dissipation magnonic devices, and on-chip quantum information processing.

From November 2023 to June 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Quantum Nano-Scale Magnetism Research Team at RIKEN, Japan, advised by Prof. Yoshichika Otani and Prof. Qiming Shao. My work combines device physics, micromagnetic and numerical modelling, microwave measurements, and nanofabrication.

Postdoctoral interests: hybrid quantum systems, cavity magnonics, quantum acoustics, spin-based quantum technologies, and on-chip quantum devices.
Research

Research directions

Hybrid magnon-photon systems

Coherent microwave conversion, nonlinear magnonic processes, and quantum-device interfaces.

Magnon-magnon coupling

Strong coupling and low dissipation in all-magnetic-insulator heterostructures.

Magnon-phonon crystals

Engineered periodic and moiré-like structures for tailoring hybrid excitations.

Acoustic-wave devices

Phononic-crystal reflectors and quality-factor enhancement in piezoelectric MEMS resonators.
