About

I am a Ph.D. student in Electronic and Computer Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Qiming Shao. From November 2023 to June 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Quantum Nano-Scale Magnetism Research Team at RIKEN, advised by Prof. Yoshichika Otani and Prof. Qiming Shao. My work combines device physics, micromagnetic and numerical modelling, microwave measurements, and nanofabrication.

Research

My research moves across quantum science, magnonics, spintronics, and acoustic devices, with a common goal: engineering interactions between physical excitations for useful on-chip functions.

Hybrid quantum systems

Coherent microwave conversion, nonlinear magnonic processes, and photon-mediated interfaces between magnons and superconducting qubits.

Magnon-magnon coupling

Strong coupling and low dissipation in all-magnetic-insulator heterostructures, studied through modelling, simulations, and experiments.

Magnon-phonon crystals

Engineered periodic and moiré-like structures for tailoring hybrid magnon-phonon excitations and their band structures.

Phononic & acoustic devices

Phononic-crystal reflectors, anchor-loss reduction, and quality-factor enhancement in piezoelectric MEMS resonators.
